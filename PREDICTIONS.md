# PREDICTIONS

**Status: NOT YET FROZEN.** This file is written and frozen in **Phase 7**, before
tapeout. Once frozen it is never edited — corrections go in a dated addendum at the
bottom. It will pre-register, with the methodology used to compute each:

- **Fmax** (OpenSTA, tt corner) and per-block setup/hold timing margins.
- **Area**: cell count and utilization on the 2×2 TT tile.
- **Expected RX BER vs injected chip-flip noise** (1e-4 … 1e-1) for DBPSK and DQPSK,
  with the ±1 dB-equivalent acceptance band vs theory.
- **SFD false-detect rate** under noise-only stimulus.

Pre-registration is the point: predictions are committed *before* the silicon/closure
results are known, then compared honestly afterward.
